### What to watch out for when adding more

- Unsplash search mixes free results with **Unsplash+ / Getty** ones, which are paid.
  The photo page states which applies — check it before downloading.
- A free licence covers the photographer's copyright only. It does **not** grant a
  model or property release, so avoid images with recognisable faces for commercial
  pages.
- Never put a stock photo in a section that claims to show this studio. That is
  misleading regardless of the licence.
